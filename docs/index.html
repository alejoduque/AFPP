SoundWalk · DerivaSonora

a soundwalk instrument · un instrumento de deriva sonora

EN — SoundWalk turns a walk into an instrument. Record geotagged sounds, trace your path as a living GPS track, annotate what you hear with fast, tap-based bioacoustic metadata, and listen back through nine playback modes — from 3D spatial audio to solar-time bridges that connect the dawn of one place with the dawn of another.

ES — DerivaSonora convierte una caminata en un instrumento. Graba sonidos geolocalizados, traza tu recorrido como un tracklog GPS vivo, anota lo que escuchas con metadatos bioacústicos rápidos de marcar, y vuelve a escuchar a través de nueve modos de reproducción — del audio espacial 3D a los puentes solares que conectan el amanecer de un lugar con el de otro.

It is two things at once: an instrument for soundwalks and dérives, and a tool for young people to become active in the conservation of their own territories through bioacoustics — mapping the birds, insects, water and voices of the places they live. Developed as doctoral research in artistic creation (Biocracia, UTADEO), grounded in the MANAKAI reserve, Córdoba, Colombia.

// what it does · qué hace

  • Geotagged field recording with tap-only bioacoustic metadata (habitat, sound stratum, distance, activity, species) — almost no typing.
  • GPS-tracked “sound drifts” (derivas) that draw themselves on the map.
  • Nine listening modes: Nearby (spatial radar), Clock, Dawn, Dusk, Strata, Chronological, Jamm, Migratory, Spectrum.
  • Share complete soundwalks as ZIP packages; export to Raven Pro, Audacity labels and GPX (QGIS/ArcGIS).
  • Bilingual (español / English) · fully offline · Android + web.

// privacy · privacidad

No server. No accounts. No analytics. No ads. Everything you record stays on your device until you export it. Privacy policy / Política de privacidad.
