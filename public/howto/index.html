How to use
Deriva Sonora

Walkers anywhere record the sounds of their derives (aimless walks) and, if they choose, share them, even live as they walk. Here they are heard and talked about: a planet with a dot wherever someone shared a derive, the street map of each place, the rings to mix distant sounds, and #AFPP, the chat. It is one app, the same in a browser and on an Android phone. A project of the Alpine Feminist Punk Parade. Three tones only: black, purple, and white for whatever is active or sounding.

The planet and the map

The planet

  • Each blinking dot is a shared derive: the more recent, the faster it blinks; white if it is from the last hour or was just shared.
  • drag to turn it. The mouse wheel or a pinch zooms; while the pointer is over the planet it stops spinning, so you can catch a dot.
  • Hover a dot to see who and how many sounds; click it to fly there.
  • Keep zooming in and the planet dives into the street map at the place you are pointing at. Zoom the map far out and the planet comes back.

The tracklog

  • When someone shares a derive, or walks one live, the planet turns into a tracklog: it flies down to the walk and plays it in time, the walked path in purple, its last steps in white, each sound a ring that lights up when the walker reaches it.
  • ♪ listen plays each sound as it is reached. ◯ planet (or Esc) goes back; ▶ tracklog replays the latest walks.
  • A live walk is followed as it happens. If you are on the street map, a line offers to watch instead of taking you away.

The street map

  • Purple line art of the place. Traces are walks, squares their sounds. Tap a trace to list its sounds, a square to hear it.
  • ▶ listens; → rings sends it to the mix. Your own derives and sounds are there too, and anyone walking live right now is a dashed white line with a blinking walker.
  • ◯ planet in the bar goes back up. ⌖ follows you again if you moved the map.
  • Every place has its own address (e.g. …/AlpineFeministPunkParade/#46.5000,8.5000): copy it to share that map.

The rings

  • Open ◎ rings. The sounds you sent wait on the edge: drag them inside the circle to hear them.
  • Closer to the centre is louder; left or right pans. Drop one outside and it waits again.
  • Sounds from far-apart places can play together: that is the point. Each one loops.
  • Keyboard: Tab to a sound, arrows to move it, Enter in or out, Delete to remove. Your mix is kept on this device.

Walk and record

The bar

  • At the bottom, always: ⌖ here (or ◯ planet on the map) · ● RECORD · ▶ START DERIVE · ≡ derives.
  • In a browser nothing asks for your location or a name until you use one of them. The first time you record or start a derive you choose an alias: it signs your derives. If you share one it is public, so it need not be your name; you can stay anonymous.

Derive and record

  • Allow location (to draw your walk) and the microphone (to record). The map flies to you; the GPS accuracy shows at the top (⌖ ±6 m): a sound needs a place.
  • ● RECORD opens the mic; tap it again (■) to stop (5 minutes at most). Recording starts a derive if none is running.
  • When you stop, name it if you like (what do you hear?) and add notes, then Keep or Discard.
  • Walk: your trace draws in white; you are the blinking square.
  • ■ END DERIVE and give it a name. If the app closed mid-walk, the derive resumes where it was.

Walk live

  • During a derive, ◍ live streams it as you walk: everyone on the site sees your path (to about a metre) and the names of your sounds, and the planet turns into your tracklog.
  • Optionally your microphone too: the people in #AFPP hear what you hear, while you walk.
  • It asks each time. Nothing is kept: when the derive ends the live walk is gone, unless you share it.

Share

  • After a derive with sounds the app offers to share it to the global map; or later, from ≡ derives.
  • The first time it explains what becomes public: your alias, the title and times, the trace (rounded to about a metre), the sounds with their names and notes. The bitácora notes that you shared it.
  • Within seconds it is a white dot on the planet, and the planet plays it as a tracklog for whoever is looking.
  • On the global map → Yes removes it from the server (trace and sounds), and from the bitácora. Deleting a derive removes it too.
  • Offline, everything stays on the device; share when there is a network.

The Android app

  1. Download deriva-sonora.apk on the phone and open it.
  2. Android asks to allow installing apps from this source (the browser or file manager): allow it for this install.
  3. The app is called Deriva Sonora. It opens on the planet and flies to you once the GPS has a fix; it never uses location in the background.
  4. No Android? The same app works in the browser: altred.xyz/AlpineFeministPunkParade in Safari or Chrome on a Mac, an iPhone or any computer. On a Mac, Location Services must be on for the browser (System Settings → Privacy & Security); on an iPhone, keep the screen on while walking. Sounds stay in that browser until you share the derive.

The chat, #AFPP

  • Beside the map (below it on a phone) is #AFPP, the chat of the Alpine Feminist Punk Parade, bridged with its Telegram group. Pick a nickname and Join. /nick renames you, /me describes an action; tap its title to fold it away.
  • Lines are typed out again at the pace they were written, hesitations and corrections included, and you see the others writing as they write (> ana writes ::).
  • voice · camera opens your microphone or camera to the people in the chat. ascii sends your camera as text only: the image never leaves your device.
  • Everything said in #AFPP, and every shared derive, is kept in the public bitácora, where lines can be played back as they were typed.

What you do not share never leaves your device. No accounts, ads or analytics. A shared derive can always be removed.
