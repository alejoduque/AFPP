How to use
Deriva Sonora

Walkers anywhere record the sounds of their derives (aimless walks) and, if they choose, share them. Here they are heard: a globe with a dot wherever someone shared a derive, the map of each place, and the rings to mix distant sounds together. A project of the Alpine Feminist Punk Parade. Three tones only: black, purple, and white for whatever is active or sounding.

Listen, on the web

The globe

  • Each blinking dot is a shared derive: the more recent, the faster it blinks; white if it is from the last hour or was just uploaded.
  • drag to turn it; the mouse wheel zooms. While the pointer is over the globe it stops spinning, so you can catch a dot.
  • Hover a dot to see who and how many sounds; click to go there.

The map of the place

  • The globe turns to the dot and lands on a map of about 10 km, drawn in purple lines.
  • Traces are the walks, squares the sounds. Tap a trace to list its sounds, a square to hear it.
  • ▶ listens; → rings sends it to the mix; ◯ Globe goes back.
  • Every place has its own address (e.g. …/AlpineFeministPunkParade/#46.5000,8.5000): copy it to share that map.

The rings

  • Open ◎ rings at the bottom. The sounds you sent wait on the edge: drag them inside the circle to hear them.
  • Closer to the centre is louder; left or right pans. Drop one outside and it waits again.
  • Sounds from far-apart places can play together: that is the point. Each one loops.
  • Keyboard: Tab to a sound, arrows to move it, Enter in or out, Delete to remove. Your mix is kept in your browser.

The chat and the bitácora

Beside the globe (below it on a phone) is #AFPP, the chat of the Alpine Feminist Punk Parade. Pick a nickname and Join. /nick renames you, /me describes an action; tap its title to fold it away. Everything said in #AFPP, and every shared derive, is kept in the public bitácora.

Record and share, with the app

Install

  1. No Android? Record in the browser instead: open altred.xyz/AlpineFeministPunkParade/?recorder in Safari or Chrome on a Mac, an iPhone or any computer, and allow location and the microphone. On a Mac, Location Services must be on for the browser (System Settings → Privacy & Security). On an iPhone, keep the screen on while walking. Sounds stay in that browser until you share the derive.
  2. On an Android phone, download deriva-sonora.apk and open it.
  3. Android asks to allow installing apps from this source (the browser or file manager): allow it for this install.
  4. The app is called Deriva Sonora.
  5. First run asks for an alias: it signs your derives. If you share one it is public, so it need not be your name. You can stay anonymous.
  6. Allow location (to draw your walk) and the microphone (to record). The app never uses location in the background.

Derive and record

  • Bottom bar: ◯ globe · ● RECORD · ≡ derives; under record, ▶ start derive.
  • Wait for the GPS accuracy at the top (⌖ ±6 m): a sound needs a place.
  • ● RECORD opens the mic; tap it again (■) to stop (5 minutes at most). Recording starts a derive if none is running.
  • When you stop, name it if you like (what do you hear?) and add notes, then Keep or Discard.
  • Walk: your trace draws in white; you are the blinking square. ⌖ follows you again if you moved the map.
  • ■ end derive and give it a name. If the app closed mid-walk, the derive resumes where it was.

Share

  • After a derive with sounds the app offers to share it to the global map; or later, from ≡ derives.
  • The first time it explains what becomes public: your alias, the title and times, the trace (rounded to about a metre), the sounds with their names and notes. The bitácora notes that you shared it.
  • Within seconds it is a white dot on the globe, for anyone.
  • On the global map → Yes removes it from the server (trace and sounds), and from the bitácora. Deleting a derive in the app removes it too.
  • Offline, everything stays on the phone; share when there is a network.

Listen to others in the app

  • On the app’s map, derives shared near you are dashed and their sounds are hollow squares: tap to listen.
  • ◯ globe opens the same globe and rings as the web; ← Recorder goes back.

What you do not share never leaves your phone. No accounts, ads or analytics. A shared derive can always be removed.
